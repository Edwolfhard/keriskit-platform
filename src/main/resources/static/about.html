Our Story

From seeing a keris,
to understanding it.

A complete keris brings together individual components, each with a place in its overall form. KerisKit creates an opportunity to explore those parts before looking at the whole.

Our approach connects a physical modular kit with digital learning materials. Learners can examine components, explore how they relate, and continue their discovery through the website.

The project focuses on the keris of the Malay Peninsula, with a learning experience built around exploration, assembly, and discovery.
